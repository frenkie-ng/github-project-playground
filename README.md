# github-project-playground

A Financial Education & Toolkit Platform for Gen Z.

## Vision
To simplify complex financial concepts into "Micro-learning" modules and power them with interactive "Smart Tools" and "Gamification" to help Gen Z start their investment journey safely.

## Core Features
- **Academy**: Practical roadmaps (Affiliate, Stock, Crypto) and safety checklists.
- **Smart Tools**: Financial calculators (Compound interest, Retirement).
- **AI Mentor**: AI-powered content summarization and guidance.
- **Smart Tracking**: Expense tracking and FOMO alerts.
- **Gamification**: Community goals and goal-based groups.
- **Roadmap**: Personalized learning paths.

## Methodology
- **Lean Startup**: Build-Measure-Learn.
- **Micro-learning**: Content in small, digestible chunks.
- **Safety First**: "Check-var" systems to prevent scams.
