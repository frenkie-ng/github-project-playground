# Agile Sprint Roadmap: Daily Tips MVP (4 Weeks)

This roadmap outlines the first 4 weeks of development focusing on a "micro-MVP" approach.

## Goals
1.  Launch Academy (Affiliate Roadmap) to build initial user base.
2.  Test AI Mentor (Article Summarization) core logic.
3.  Deploy Smart Tracking (OCR) as a beta feature.

---

## 📅 Week 1: Foundation & Academy Startup
*Focus on EPIC-001 & EPIC-006 (Auth)*

- **Core**: Auth (Biometric) & Profile Setup.
- **Feature**: US-001.001 (Affiliate Roadmap) - Content & UI.
- **Project**: Configure Project Board v2 with automatic filters.

## 📅 Week 2: AI Mentor & Micro-learning
*Focus on EPIC-003 & EPIC-001*

- **Feature**: US-003.001 (AI Summarizer) - API & UI.
- **Feature**: US-001.002 (Scam Audit) - Logic & Colors.
- **Gamification**: Setup XP Backend awarding logic.

## 📅 Week 3: Smart Tracking Beta
*Focus on EPIC-004*

- **Feature**: US-004.001 (Bill OCR) - Integration with S3/Tesseract.

## 📅 Week 4: Community & Gamification
*Focus on EPIC-005 & EPIC-002*
