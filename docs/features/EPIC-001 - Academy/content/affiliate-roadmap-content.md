# Affiliate Marketing Roadmap: From 0 to 100k VND

Welcome to your 7-day kickstart! No capital, no boss, just your phone and a bit of "hành động".

---

## Day 1: The "Hustle" Mindset
Affiliate marketing is simply being a digital matchmaker. You recommend products you love, and get a cut (commission) when someone buys.
- **Why Gen Z?**: You already know how to make things go viral. Why not get paid for it?
- **Goal**: Understand that you don't need to "own" the product to "own" the profit.

**✅ Checklist**:
- [ ] Research 3 people on TikTok making money with Affiliate.
- [ ] Choose your vibe: Reviewer, Tutorial maker, or Lifestyle.

---

## Day 2: Choose Your Niche (Don't Be a Generalist)
Don't sell everything. Be the "go-to" person for one thing.
- **Student Ideas**: Tech gadgets for study, budget fashion, or healthy "tiệm tạp hóa" snacks.
- **Gen Z Tip**: If you love it, your followers will too.

**✅ Checklist**:
- [ ] Pick 1 niche you are genuinely interested in.
- [ ] Verify if there are student-budget products in this niche.

---

## Day 3: Find Your Platforms
Where does your tribe hang out?
- **TikTok/Reels**: Best for fast reach and "unboxing" vibes.
- **Threads/Facebook Groups**: Best for detailed "review" threads.

**✅ Checklist**:
- [ ] Create/Optimize 1 social media profile specifically for your niche.
- [ ] Add a clean Bio (no links yet!).

---

## Day 4: Finding Your First Product
Go where the big players are (Shopee, Lazada, or specialized Tech platforms).
- **Rule**: Only pick products with 4.5+ star reviews. Your reputation is everything.

**✅ Checklist**:
- [ ] Sign up for 1 Affiliate Network (e.g., Shopee Affiliate).
- [ ] Select 3 products within your niche to promote.

---

## Day 5: Content That Actually Converts
Stop "selling", start "solving".
- **Hook**: Start with a problem. "How I study 8 hours without burning out..."
- **Value**: Show the product in action.
- **CTA**: Tell them where to find it.

**✅ Checklist**:
- [ ] Draft 1 video script or 1 post thread for your first product.
- [ ] Mention one "secret" feature of the product.

---

## Day 6: The "Bio Link" Strategy
You can't put links everywhere. You need a "hub".
- **Tools**: Linktree, Beacons, or a simple landing page.

**✅ Checklist**:
- [ ] Set up your Link-in-Bio tool.
- [ ] Add your first 3 affiliate links with clear, catchy titles.

---

## Day 7: Launch & The 100k Goal
Time to post! Don't worry about "likes", focus on "clicks".
- **The 100k VND Milestone**: Your first few sales will prove it works. Consistency is the only "cheat code".

**✅ Checklist**:
- [ ] Post your first piece of affiliate content.
- [ ] Join a community group to share your results and ask for feedback.
